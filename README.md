One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened
today — written to make the judge smile.

- `main.py` — the entrypoint. Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line: a warung lady slips an extra krupuk into the bag with a whispered
"for the road", and her kindness gets carried all the way to work — a small
kindness, one concrete detail, and a turn at the end.
